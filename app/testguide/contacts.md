1. Open All Contacts and click anywhere on a card. => The big card opens.
2. Click outside the big card. => It closes; an open menu inside closes first.
3. Edit a contact and save. => The new value shows, and its source says “edited by” you.
4. (v1.0) On the big card click Company Page. => The Company page opens for that company.
5. (v1.0) Check the All Contacts list. => Contacts of the Test Directory are not shown.
