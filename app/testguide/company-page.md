Open a contact that has a website, then Company Page.

1. (v1.0) Look at the Details table. => Each detail shows how it was found, the page link, the date found and last checked.
2. (v1.0) Click Verify All Details. => A Verify request runs; after reloading, each detail shows Still the same, Changed or Not found.
3. (v1.0) For a Changed detail click Accept New Value. => The new value is saved and History says who accepted it.
4. (v1.0) For another changed detail click Keep Old. => The old value stays and is marked Kept.
5. (v1.0) Click Make Profile & Products. => After it finishes the Profile shows type, categories, main products, and the Products list fills.
6. (v1.0) Add a product by hand with a category, then remove one. => The list changes; NetGate's own products are replaced next time, yours stay.
7. (v1.0) Open Settings → Product Categories, add a line and save. => The new category shows in the Add a Product list.
8. (v1.0) Set Night Jobs → Verify contacts not checked for 6 months and save. => The value stays after reloading.
