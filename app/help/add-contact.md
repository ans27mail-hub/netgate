Press **+ Add Contact** at the top.

## One contact
Type the name and whatever you know: city, country, links (website, eBay / Etsy / Amazon store, Instagram, Facebook — one per line), email, phone and notes. NetGate recognises each link and puts it in the right place.

While you type, **Already in NetGate?** shows companies that look the same. If an exact match exists (same eBay seller, same website, or same name and city), your details are **added to that contact** instead of creating a duplicate.

## Paste a list
One company per line: `name, city, country, website` — leave out what you don't know. Commas, semicolons and tabs work, so you can paste straight from Excel. Emails and phone numbers on a line are recognised.

## Import CSV
The first row must have column names. NetGate understands *name* (or company, store, shop), *city*, *country*, *website*, *email*, *phone*, *notes*, and any column named like a field of the chosen directory (e.g. *Seller rating*). From Excel: File → Save as → CSV.

## Then
- **Intended directory** decides which fields to look for (or *Not sure yet* for common fields only).
- **Enrich with** — tick the steps to run (see [Enrich](/help/enrich)).
- **Save & Enrich Now**, **Save to Inbox Only**, or **Save & Enrich Tonight**.
- People who may review can tick **Accept into the directory straight away** to skip the Inbox.
