Open **Directories** and press the **+ New Directory** card (or Settings → **+ New Directory**).

1. **Basics** — a name (e.g. *Online retailers*) and a short description.
2. **Search methods** — tick one or more (see [Search methods](/help/search-methods)). For eBay choose the marketplaces (ebay.co.uk, ebay.de …), how many listings to read per keyword, a minimum feedback score and *Business sellers only*. For directory sites give their list pages or site names.
3. **Fields** — tick the starting fields. When eBay is on, *Sells on*, *Seller rating*, *Feedback count* and *Business name* are filled straight from eBay.
4. **Instructions for the AI** — in plain language: what belongs, how to fill the fields, what to ignore.
5. **Keywords** — the searches to run, and whether they run at night.

Press **Create Directory**. You land on its settings page to fine-tune everything; results of its searches arrive in the Inbox marked *for* this directory.
