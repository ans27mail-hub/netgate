Every company a search finds — and every contact added by hand — first lands in the **Inbox**. Nothing reaches a directory until someone accepts it.

## The tabs
- **New** — waiting for a decision.
- **Enriched** — waiting, and Enrich has already filled their details (handy to decide with more information).
- **Rejected** — the restore list. Rejected companies stay here; press **Restore** to bring one back.

## The keyword rows
At the top you see each directory's keywords, grouped by search method, e.g. *eBay search: leather motorcycle jacket → for Online retailers*. Click one to run it now.

## Deciding
- **Accept** puts the company into the directory it was found for.
- **Accept into** (in the bar that appears when you tick rows) puts it into another directory. One company can be in several directories.
- **Reject…** asks for a reason (Private seller, Trader, Wrong country…). The reason teaches the suggestions.
- **Enrich** fills missing details first, if you need more to decide.

Click a row to open the full card.

## “AI suggests”
NetGate compares each new company with the ones you accepted and rejected before for the same directory, e.g. *Online retailers 94% · like 23 you accepted* or *Reject 71% · like 6 you rejected as private seller*. With only a few decisions it says *not enough decisions yet*. Press **Refresh Suggestions** after many decisions. See [AI suggestions](/help/ai-suggestions).

## Good to know
- A rejected company is not put in the Inbox again when a later search finds it for the same directory.
- A company that is already in a directory is not added to its Inbox again; a new search only fills its missing details.
- Filters and search at the top work the same as on All contacts.
