## 1. Log in
Use the username and password your admin gave you. What you can see and do depends on your **role** (for example *Researcher* or *Viewer*).

## 2. Look at a directory
NetGate opens on the [Dashboard](/help/dashboard). Open **Directories** on the left and click a directory, e.g. **Manufacturers**. Each card is one company. Click a card to open it: you see all its details, where each one came from, and its history.

## 3. Find new companies
Open the **Inbox** and click one of the keyword chips at the top, or press **+ Run Discovery** and type what you are looking for. The search runs in the background; the **AI jobs running** button at the top shows its progress.

## 4. Decide in the Inbox
New companies appear in the Inbox with an **AI suggestion**.
- Tick the good ones and press **Accept** (they go into the directory).
- Tick the bad ones and press **Reject…**, then pick a reason.

You can accept a company into a different directory with **Accept into**.

## 5. Fill missing details
Press the round-arrow button on a card (or **Enrich** in the Inbox). NetGate reads the company's website and other pages and fills what is missing.

## 6. Add a company you already know
Press **+ Add Contact** at the top. Type what you know (name, links, email…), choose the directory and press **Save & Enrich Now**.

## 7. Keep an eye on searches
**Requests** lists every search. You can pause, stop, delete or reject them there.

That's it. When something is unclear, press **?** at the top of the page, or ask a question on the Help home.
