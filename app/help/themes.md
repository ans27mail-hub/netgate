Settings → **Themes** (needs *Display & themes for everyone*):
- **Theme for everyone** — the one people see by default. **Let each person pick their own** — then everyone can choose their own **My Look** under Settings → Display. **Use Dark when the computer is in dark mode** — follows the computer's setting.
- **Use** makes a theme the one for everyone; **Try for Me** changes it only for you.
- **Colour sets** like BrandPartners: **A · Navy and Amber**, **B · Graphite and Racing Red**, **C · Deep Teal and Copper**, **D · Charcoal and Blue** — plus **NetGate Classic** and **Dark**.
- **Copy** a theme to make your own, then **Edit**: **Start from a colour set** fills in all colours, then change any of the 8 colours (menu, menu text, page, cards, lines, text, grey text, accent); a **text font** and a **heading font**; corners and spacing. The preview changes as you pick colours. NetGate warns when text would be hard to read; button text turns dark on a light accent by itself.
- Built-in themes can't be changed; copy them instead.

## Fonts
14 fonts are included (Instrument Sans, Archivo, Public Sans, IBM Plex Sans, Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Lato, Open Sans, Source Sans 3, Playfair Display, Source Serif 4, Noto Sans with Urdu). **Install Font** adds your own: a .woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf file up to 3 MB, kept on your server so it works without internet. It then appears in every font list.

## My Look (Settings → Display)
Everyone can pick **their own** look, when *Let each person pick their own* is on:
- **Colours** — one of the colour sets or themes, or *The Look for Everyone*.
- **Text font** and **Heading font** — or the colours' own fonts.
- **Text size** — Smaller, Normal, Larger, Largest.

**Save My Look** — only you see it. **Use the Look for Everyone** goes back.

A theme changes only colours and sizes — the layout stays the same.
