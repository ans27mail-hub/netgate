Tags are labels for a contact's status, for example **Hot lead**, **Contacted**, **Sample sent**, **Customer**, **Not interested**, **Do not contact**. A contact can have several.

## On the cards
- Each tag shows as a small coloured bar beside the round Enrich button. **Hover** it to see the name; **click** it to show only contacts with that tag.
- The card takes a light colour from its first tag (the one highest in the tag list). Cards without tags stay white.
- Above the cards, the **Tags** bar shows every tag in use with its count. Click one to filter; click again to clear. You can also add **Tag** as a filter (+ Add Filter) and sort by **Tag Order**.

## Putting tags on contacts
- Open the contact's big card → **+ Tag** → tick the tags → **Save Tags**. Press × on a tag to take it off.
- For many contacts: **List** view, tick them, choose a tag at the top → **Add Tag** or **Remove Tag**.
- The contact's history notes who tagged it and when.

## Managing tags
Settings → **Tags** (needs *Manage tags*):
- **+ New Tag**: name, meaning (shown when you hover), colour (10 colours or your own), where it can be used (all directories or only some), and whether it colours the card.
- **↑ ↓** change the order: the higher tag's colour wins on the card.
- **Merge** moves all its contacts to another tag; **Delete Tag** only takes it off the contacts (they stay).
