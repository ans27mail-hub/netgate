Open a contact and click **Company Page**.

## Details
Every field with its value and:
- **How found** — read from a page (with the page link), typed in by a person, added by hand / CSV, or eBay.
- **Found** — when this value was first saved. **Last checked** — when Verify last found it still there.
- **Verify** — the result of the last check.

## Verify
**Verify All Details** (or **Verify** on one row) opens again — fresh, not from memory — the page each detail came from, and checks it is still there:
- **Still the same** — nothing to do.
- **Changed** — the page now says something else; the new value is shown. Click **Accept New Value** or **Keep Old**.
- **Not found on the page any more** / **Page could not be read** — click **Remove It** or **Keep Old**.

Nothing changes until a person decides. Details typed in by hand are not checked (“Not from a page”).
**Night Jobs → Verify contacts not checked for … months** lets the night run do this by itself (the decisions still wait for you here). A small **Changed?** link on the contact card shows a detail waiting for a decision.

## Profile and products
**Make Profile & Products** reads the company's website (home page and a few product or about pages) and the local AI writes a short profile: what kind of company, product categories, main products, materials, markets, certifications. It also lists the **products** it found, with a picture link when the website has one. Check it before you rely on it.
- **+ Add a Product** adds one by hand; **Remove** deletes one. When the profile is made again, NetGate's own products are replaced but the ones people added stay.
- The categories come from **Settings → Product Categories** — one per line, edit them for your business.
