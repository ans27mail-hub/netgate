**All contacts** shows every accepted company, from every directory you may see.

## Filters
Press **+ Add filter** and pick one:
- **Directory**, **Country**, **City**
- **Has** email / phone / WhatsApp / website / social links
- **Completeness** (complete, 50% or more, missing fields)
- **Date added**, **Found by keyword**, **Found by method**
- fields of a directory (on a directory's page), e.g. *Sells on* or *Employees*

Each filter shows as a black chip; press × on a chip to remove it, or **Clear all**.

## Saved views
Set up filters, then press **Save view** and give it a name, e.g. *UK shops with email*. It appears under **Saved views** on the left. Press × next to it to remove it.

## Grid and list
**Grid** shows cards. **List** shows a table: tick rows to **Enrich selected** or **Add to directory**, and use **Columns** to choose what you see. **Export CSV** downloads what is shown.

## A contact's card
Click a card or row. You see every field and where it came from, the directories it is in (× removes it from one; the contact stays), store and social links, notes, and its **History** (how and when it was found, accepted or edited). **Edit** lets you change fields and notes by hand.
